Chapter 1: Prologue, Woodland Lore & Objective

PAGE 02

Deep beneath the autumn canopy of the Old Kingdom, the Queen has issued a royal proclamation: whoever outwits the creatures of the deep woods shall be named Grand Royal Advisor. Alongside the hardworking Woodcutter, the graceful Swan, the cunning Fox, the mysterious Witch, and the crowned Monarch, two rivals clash across thirteen tricks of wits and deception.

Yet beware the Queen’s ancient warning: those who grasp too greedily for every prize in the forest shall leave with empty pockets. True mastery lies in balancing ambition with humility.

! VICTORY & DEFEAT CONDITIONS !

Match Victory Target: Play proceeds across multiple 13-trick rounds. At the end of each round, players earn points based on the number of tricks won plus bonus points from captured 7 (Treasure) cards.

Winning the Game: The match ends at the conclusion of any round in which at least one player reaches or exceeds 21 Points (configurable to 16 or 35 in the Options menu). The player with the highest total score wins!

The Greed Trap (Defeat Condition): Winning 10 to 13 tricks in a single round brands you GREEDY, awarding 0 Trick Points for that round!

  5. Ability Rules Text: Summarizes whether the effect triggers immediately upon play (Fox, Woodcutter), during lead validation (Monarch), during trump comparison (Witch), or upon trick resolution (Swan, Treasure).
  6. Rarity & Deck Slot Metadata: Every card in the 33-card deck is a 1-of-1 unique rank-suit combination.

⌨ MOUSE & KEYBOARD CONTROLLER HINTS

Mouse Controls: Click and drag any legal (bright) card from your hand into the central Trick Play Well, or simply click a legal card to play it immediately. Attempting to drag an illegal (dimmed) card triggers a smooth spring bounce-back to your hand.

Keyboard Shortcuts: Press S to Sort Hand by Suit, V to Sort Hand by Value, Z to Undo the last turn, F1 to open the in-game Rules Reference, and SPACE / ENTER to advance trick resolution or reveal hands in 1v1 Hotseat mode.

Chapter 4: Step-by-Step Turn Sequence & Priority Rules

PAGE 08
  1. Phase 1 — Round Setup & Decree Determination: Shuffle all 33 cards using the deterministic PRNG. Deal 13 cards to Player 1 and 13 cards to Player 2. Place the remaining 7 cards face-down as the Stock pile, then flip the top card of the Stock pile face-up onto the Decree Slot (leaving 6 face-down cards in the Stock). The suit of the Decree card establishes the active Trump Suit. The non-dealer leads Trick #1.
  2. Phase 2 — Lead Step (WAIT_LEAD): The active lead player plays any 1 card from their hand face-up into the Lead Slot. That card's printed suit becomes the Led Suit for the trick.
  3. Phase 3 — Lead Ability Resolution (Priority Window A): If the led card is a 3 (Fox) or 5 (Woodcutter), its on-play ability resolves immediately before the opponent selects a follow card:
    • Important Timing Rule: If the lead player plays a 3 (Fox) and swaps the Decree card to a new suit, the newly swapped Decree suit becomes trump for the current trick immediately!
  4. Phase 4 — Follow Step (WAIT_FOLLOW): The following player must play 1 legal card from their hand into the Follow Slot:
    • Standard Follow Rule: If the follower holds at least one card matching the Led Suit, they must play a card of the Led Suit.
    • Monarch (11) Override: If the lead card is an 11 (Monarch) and the follower holds cards of the Led Suit, the follower is restricted to playing either the 1 (Swan) of that suit or their highest-ranked card of that suit.
    • Void in Suit: If the follower holds zero cards of the Led Suit, they may play any card from their hand (including a Trump card or off-suit dump).
  5. Phase 5 — Follow Ability Resolution (Priority Window B): If the follow card is a 3 (Fox) or 5 (Woodcutter), the follower resolves the swap or draw-and-bury modal before trick comparison occurs.
  6. Phase 6 — Trick Comparison & Winner Resolution (TRICK_RESOLVE):
    • Witch (9) Check: If exactly one 9 was played in the trick, treat that 9's suit as the current Decree (Trump) suit. If both players played a 9, their magic cancels out and both retain their printed suits.
    • Determine Winner: The highest card of the active Trump suit wins the trick. If neither card is Trump (or effective Trump), the highest card of the Led Suit wins the trick.
    • Award Treasure (7): The winner of the trick immediately receives +1 Bonus Point for each 7 present in the trick (up to +2 points if two 7s were played).
    • Determine Next Leader & Swan (1) Check: Normally, the winner of the trick leads the next trick. However, if the losing player played a 1 (Swan), the losing player steals the lead for the next trick!
  7. Phase 7 — End-of-Round Scoring (ROUND_OVER): Once all 13 tricks have been played, compare each player's total tricks won against the Scoring Brackets, add their accumulated 7 (Treasure) bonus points, and check if either player has reached 21 points.

Chapter 5: Core Mechanics, Scoring Tiers & Keyword Glossary

PAGE 10

Official Keyword Glossary

1. DECREE (Active Trump Suit)
The face-up card beside the Stock pile. Any card matching the Decree's suit outranks all cards of non-Decree suits.
2. SWAN [Rank 1] (Lead Takeover)
The lowest rank in every suit, yet tactically vital: losing a trick with a 1 grants you the lead on the next trick.
3. FOX [Rank 3] (Decree Manipulation)
Allows mid-round trump switching by swapping a card from your hand with the face-up Decree card.
4. WOODCUTTER [Rank 5] (Hand Filtering / Bury)
Draws the top card of the Stock pile and forces you to bury 1 card from your hand to the bottom of the Stock.
5. TREASURE [Rank 7] (Point Bounty)
Grants +1 permanent point to whichever player wins the trick containing it, independent of trick-tier brackets.
6. WITCH [Rank 9] (Shapeshifting Trump)
Counts as part of the Trump suit during trick resolution so long as only one 9 is in the trick.
7. MONARCH [Rank 11] (Royal Compulsion)
When led, forces a suit-following opponent to surrender either their 1 (Swan) or their highest card in that suit.
8. HUMBLE vs. GREEDY (Scoring Polarity)
Winning 0–3 tricks (Humble) awards the same maximum 6 points as winning 7–9 tricks (Victorious), while winning 10–13 tricks (Greedy) collapses your trick score to 0 points.

★ PRO-TIP: DECK & HAND MANAGEMENT

Deck Constraints: Because there are 33 cards and only 26 are dealt initially (13 per player), 6 cards remain unseen in the face-down Stock pile (unless cycled via 5 Woodcutter). There is no pre-round mulligan; instead, use the 3 Fox and 5 Woodcutter abilities to sculpt weak hands toward a 6-point Humble (0–3 tricks) finish!
